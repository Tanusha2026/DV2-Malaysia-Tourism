Malaysia and Tourism

Malaysia on the Move

Tourism recovery, returning visitors, and the states benefiting most.

Section 1

Has tourism recovered?

Has tourism recovered?

Malaysia’s tourism recovery is indexed against the 2019 baseline. After a sharp fall in 2020 and 2021, arrivals and receipts recovered strongly from 2022 onward. By 2024, receipts had risen above the 2019 level, while arrivals were close to fully recovering.

How strong was domestic tourism?

Domestic tourism also strengthened in 2024. Visitor arrivals rose from 213.7 million to 260.1 million, while domestic spending increased from RM84.9 billion to RM106.7 billion.

Section 2

When do visitors arrive?

When did visitors arrive?

Monthly arrivals show the rhythm of tourism recovery. Visitor numbers were consistently higher in 2024, with December recording the strongest arrival volume.

Where is tourism concentrated in Malaysia?

Where were hotel guests located?

Hotel guest activity was geographically concentrated across major tourism states. The map allows domestic, foreign, and total hotel guest patterns to be compared interactively.

Where did hotel activity concentrate?

Hotel guest activity was strongest in major urban and tourism destinations. Kuala Lumpur recorded the highest total, while Pahang stood out for domestic hotel guests.

Section 4

Which states show different tourism profiles?

Were states domestic-led or foreign-led?

State-level hotel activity was not uniform. Kuala Lumpur combined high domestic and foreign hotel demand, while Pahang stood out as a more domestic-led destination.

How did major states differ?

The selected states show different tourism profiles. Kuala Lumpur performed strongly in total and foreign hotel guest activity, while Pahang was more domestic-oriented.

Section 5

Who is returning to Malaysia?

Where did visitors come from?

Malaysia’s leading source markets were concentrated in nearby Asian countries, especially Singapore and Indonesia. The map also shows China’s stronger return in 2024.

Which source markets shifted?

Singapore and Indonesia remained Malaysia’s two largest source markets. China moved into third place in 2024, while Thailand shifted down one rank.

Section 6

What do visitors spend on?

What powered the spending rebound?

Most spending categories increased in 2024. Shopping, food, accommodation, and international airfares made the largest contributions to the recovery.
